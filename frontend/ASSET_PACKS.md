# Free Pixel Art Asset Packs For Florecer

## Top 3 Picks Overall

1. **Tiny RPG - Mana Soul GUI**  
   Best UI fit: CC0, free, pixel-art panels/buttons/bars, and already close to a warm RPG companion-app interface. It includes buttons, equipment frames, 9-slice frames, bars, cursors, minimap/portrait/item frames, and is “Name your own price.” [Source](https://tiopalada.itch.io/tiny-rpg-mana-soul-gui)

2. **Shikashi’s Fantasy Icons Pack (FREE)**  
   Strongest savings-goal icon base among verified packs: CC-BY 4.0, free, 284 32x32 icons, includes brain, instruments, tools, coins, books, plant-pot stages, and other reward/progress-adjacent icons. Attribution required. [Source](https://shikashipx.itch.io/shikashis-fantasy-icons-pack)

3. **Brackeys’ VFX Bundle**  
   Best celebration/VFX source: CC0, free, includes particle textures, flipbooks, and spritesheets for glow, burst, fire, smoke, and magical effects that can be repurposed for level-up auras and reward moments. [Source](https://brackeysgames.itch.io/brackeys-vfx-bundle)

### PIXEL FANTASY RPG ICONS - Complete Free Collection
- **URL:** https://cazwolf.itch.io/caz-pixel-free
- **Author:** Caz
- **License:** CC-BY 4.0 per author comment on the page; attribution required.

**Gap note:** I did not find a verified CC0/CC-BY free pack on itch/OpenGameArt that clearly covers all requested modern savings icons: laptop, phone, computer, motorbike/scooter, rocket, and microphone. Shikashi and Caz cover some adjacent needs, especially brain, tools, music/instruments, coins, books, buttons, and general rewards.

## 4. Plant / Seedling / Flower Growth Sprites

**Verified gap:** I found several free plant/growing-plant packs, but the strong matches I checked did **not** clearly state CC0 or CC-BY on the actual page. For example, Danaida’s growing-plants pack is free and has growth frames, but its page uses a custom license rather than CC0/CC-BY, so I excluded it under your hard requirement.

**Best usable plant-adjacent assets inside included packs:**
- **Shikashi’s Fantasy Icons Pack** includes plant-pot seedling/growing/fully-grown icons, but these are 32x32 icons rather than companion sprites.
- **Caz Complete Free Collection** includes farm-tool and RPG icon content, but not a verified multi-stage companion plant sprite set.

## 5. Layered Character Generator Packs

**Verified gap:** I did not find a genuinely free, layered pixel-art character generator pack on itch/OpenGameArt that both met the target style and clearly used CC0 or CC-BY.

Near misses excluded:
- **Mana Seed Character Base** has an excellent paper-doll system, but the full layered system requires payment and does not present CC0/CC-BY licensing.
- **Pixel Gnome Customizable Playable Character Pack** has good customization, but the customizable layered pack is gated at $10+ and uses a custom license, not CC0/CC-BY.
- **LuckyLoops Fully Animated Character Template** is free and CC-BY 4.0, but it is a single animated template, not a layered generator with hats/clothes/accessories.
